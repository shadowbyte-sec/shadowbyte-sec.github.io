INDEPENDENT LEARNING · PROJECT ROADMAP

Project briefs.

The following are proposed learning demonstrations using entirely fictional customer and IT scenarios. They are not completed projects or prior employer work.

01 / IT Help Desk Ticket Simulator

Goal: create a simple sample service board with fields for issue, urgency, impact, priority, assigned role, status, and follow-up notes. Practice deciding what information a technician needs before escalation.

02 / Troubleshooting Knowledge Base

Goal: publish concise, customer-friendly how-to guides for common issues such as account access, MFA, and connectivity. Include verification and security reminders without exposing private data.

03 / Bilingual Support Scenarios

Goal: document fictional customer conversations in English and Spanish, with summaries, next steps, and sample internal notes. Demonstrate clarity, empathy, and attention to detail.
